# WhatsApp Bot Warroom

Shared language for the bot's operational overview and the team's work overview.

## Language

**Warroom**:
An overview of both the bot's operational status and the team's work status. The bot's operational status is the first area to be designed.

**Bot status**:
The current operational condition of the WhatsApp bot, including what is known and not known about whether it can work.

**WhatsApp connection**:
The bot's reported connection to WhatsApp. A connection alone does not establish that the bot can reply to messages.

**Verified reply**:
Evidence that the bot has replied; its time and the scope of the verification must be clear.

**Reply sent**:
A reply whose send operation has completed successfully. This alone is not confirmation that it reached its recipient.

**Delivery confirmed**:
An explicit acknowledgement that a reply reached its recipient. It does not establish that the recipient read it.

**Alert pause**:
A temporary suspension of notifications until a specified time. It does not mean the bot is healthy or that the bot itself is paused.

**Last received status**:
The most recent status the Warroom received from the bot, together with when it was received. It may no longer describe the bot's current condition.

**Team work status**:
An overview of the work tracked for the team, including outstanding tasks and their progress.

**My tasks (งานของฉัน)**:
Tasks assigned to the signed-in member within the WhatsApp groups they can access.

**Group tasks (งานในกลุ่ม)**:
Tasks in the WhatsApp groups the signed-in member can access, including tasks assigned to teammates and tasks without an assignee.
